2The Council

Five agents reviewed the live site as evidence and debated across 3 rounds. Final positions distilled below.

🏛️Serena · Architect

"A near-flat namespace masquerading as an information architecture — filesystem folders are not a taxonomy." Model Teacher / Source / Concept as real entities; dedup + stable IDs before the publish wave, because indexed URLs are immutable.

🎨Aditi · Designer

"This site wears a developer's clothes to a meditation." Kill the chrome; contemplative typography, one centered column, a serif with soul. Truncated titles & duplicates are unforgivable. Pick one voice. The words are the design.

⚙️Marcus · Engineer

The static Python link-scripts guarantee drift — stale pages, duplicates, the draft→404 footgun. Killing the chrome is "an afternoon in quartz.layout.ts — highest ROI." Commit the cleaned 647; buy the cheap 80%.

🔍Ava · Researcher

Attribution is the SEO moat (Goodreads: stable URL + source + tags). Ritual drives retention — a daily-quote permalink, à la The Marginalian. "Depth is the moat, not cards. Card converts strangers; concept-threads retain seekers."

🌱Dev · First-time visitor

"Beautiful words, but who said this? No author, no breadcrumb, no next-quote — I'd bounce in four seconds." The chrome "looks like someone's private notebook I wasn't supposed to see." Wants attribution + "give me another" everywhere.

"Build the moat, ship the doorknob first." — the council's converged sequencing

Convergence: clean before publish (stable IDs before indexing) · attribution ships now, not "year three" · kill the developer chrome · card-then-depth as the funnel.
Remaining tension: how much of the formal entity model to build now (Marcus/Dev: defer; Serena/Ava: model it correctly even if rendered quietly).
