## 2. Conscious Immortality

- **Author:** Paul Brunton and Munagala S. Venkataramiah
- **Full Title:** Conscious Immortality: Conversations with Sri Ramana Maharshi
- **Description:** A record of conversations and observations made during visits to Sri Ramana Maharshi in the 1930s; over half the content also appears in *Talks with Sri Ramana Maharshi*.
- **Free Reading Link:** https://archive.org/details/consciousimmortalitypaulbrunton_701_b

## 4. Vidyeshvara Samhita (Shiva Purana)

- **Author:** Vyasa (traditional attribution); English translation by J.L. Shastri (Motilal Banarsidass edition)
- **Full Title:** Vidyeshvara Samhita — the first of seven sections (samhitas) of the Shiva Purana
- **Description:** The opening section of the Shiva Purana dedicated to describing the greatness and devotion (bhakti) of Lord Shiva, particularly through the worship of the Shiva Linga.
- **Free Reading Links:**
  - WisdomLib (chapter-by-chapter online): https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/shiva-purana-english/d/doc225543.html
  - Archive.org (J.L. Shastri translation, Part 1 of 4): https://archive.org/details/SivaPuranaJ.L.ShastriPart1
- **URL Status:** BOTH VERIFIED (200)

## 5. Bhagavad Gita

- **Author:** Vyasa (traditional attribution); many translators
- **Description:** The 700-verse Hindu scripture in which Lord Krishna counsels Arjuna on duty, action, devotion, and knowledge on the battlefield of Kurukshetra.
- **Free Reading Links:**
  - Gita Press edition (Archive.org): https://archive.org/details/gitapress-gita-roman
  - With Shankaracharya commentary, tr. Alladi Mahadeva Sastri (Archive.org): https://archive.org/details/Bhagavad-Gita.with.the.Commentary.of.Sri.Shankaracharya
- **URL Status:** BOTH VERIFIED (200)

## 6. Tripura Rahasya

- **Author:** Attributed to Haritayana (ancient sage); English translation by Swami Sri Ramananda Saraswathi (recommended by Ramana Maharshi)
- **Full Title:** Tripura Rahasya: The Mystery Beyond the Trinity
- **Description:** An ancient Advaitic text in dialogue form revealing the nature of consciousness and ultimate reality; highly recommended by Sri Ramana Maharshi as one of the greatest works on non-dual philosophy.
- **Free Reading Link:** https://archive.org/details/TripuraRahasyaEnglishTranslation
- **URL Status:** VERIFIED (200)

## 7. Upadesa Saram

- **Author:** Sri Ramana Maharshi
- **Full Title:** Upadesa Saram (Sanskrit) / Upadesa Undiyar (Tamil) — "The Essence of Instruction"
- **Description:** Thirty verses composed by Ramana Maharshi summarizing the essence of his spiritual teachings on action, devotion, yoga, and knowledge as paths to Self-realization.
- **Free Reading Links:**
  - Archive.org (B.V. Narasimhaswami translation): https://archive.org/details/Acc.No.6193UpadesaSaram1970
  - David Godman PDF (Upadesa Undiyar): https://www.davidgodman.org/rteach/Upadesa_Undiyar.pdf
- **URL Status:** BOTH VERIFIED (200)

## 8. The Prophet

- **Author:** Kahlil Gibran
- **Full Title:** The Prophet (1923)
- **Description:** A prose-poetry masterpiece in which the prophet Almustafa offers wisdom on love, marriage, work, joy, sorrow, freedom, beauty, death, and other universal themes as he prepares to depart the city of Orphalese.
- **Free Reading Links:**
  - Project Gutenberg (HTML full text): https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/58585
  - Archive.org (1923 Knopf edition): https://archive.org/details/prophet00gibr_1
- **URL Status:** BOTH VERIFIED (200)

## 9. In Search of the Miraculous

- **Author:** P.D. Ouspensky
- **Full Title:** In Search of the Miraculous: Fragments of an Unknown Teaching
- **Description:** Ouspensky's account of his years studying with G.I. Gurdjieff, documenting the Fourth Way system of self-development including the enneagram, centers of consciousness, and "the Work."
- **Free Reading Link:** https://archive.org/details/insearchofmiracu0000uspe
