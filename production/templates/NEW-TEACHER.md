# New Teacher Template

> Copy the frontmatter and structure below to `content/Teachers/{Teacher Name}.md` and fill in.

## Frontmatter

```yaml
---
draft: false
title: "Teacher Name"
tradition: "e.g., Advaita Vedanta, Zen, Sufism"
period: "e.g., 1879-1950"
lineage: "Brief lineage or transmission context"
core_teaching: "One-sentence summary of their central teaching"
image: "filename.jpg"
recommended_start: "[[Book Title]]"
---
```

### Field Reference

| Field | Required | Description |
|-------|----------|-------------|
| `draft` | Yes | `false` to publish, `true` to hide |
| `title` | Yes | Teacher's full name — becomes the page title and wiki-link target |
| `tradition` | Yes | Primary spiritual tradition |
| `period` | Yes | Lifespan or active period |
| `lineage` | No | Guru lineage, transmission context, or "Spontaneous self-realization" |
| `core_teaching` | Yes | One sentence summarizing their central message |
| `image` | No | Filename of portrait in `content/Teachers/Images/` (see Image section below) |
| `recommended_start` | No | Wiki-link to the best introductory book |

## Page Structure

```markdown
> *"One-line essence of the teacher's message."*

## Who Was [Teacher Name]?

Brief biography — 2-3 paragraphs covering life, awakening, and significance.

## Core Teaching

What is the central message? What practice or understanding did they emphasize?

## Tradition & Lineage

Where do they sit in the stream of transmission?

[Official website or resource](https://link)

## Where to Start

If you're new to this teacher, begin with [[Recommended Book]] — brief description of why.

## Passages

- [[Quote 1]]
- [[Quote 2]]
```

### Section Notes

- **Opening quote** — A single representative quote in blockquote format
- **Who Was** — Accessible biography for someone who's never heard of the teacher. Include dates, location, key life events
- **Core Teaching** — The essential practice or insight. Use bold for key terms
- **Tradition & Lineage** — Spiritual context. Link to official resources if available
- **Where to Start** — Recommend ONE book as the entry point, with a sentence on why
- **Passages** — Wiki-links to quotes attributed to this teacher. Auto-generated by `scripts/generate-book-links.py` when quotes have matching `source` or `Author` fields

## Image Guidelines

- Place portrait images in `content/Teachers/Images/`
- Preferred: Wikimedia Commons images (public domain / CC-BY)
- Naming: `wikimedia_commons_{hash}_{description}.jpg`
- Reference in frontmatter as just the filename (no path)
- The `ArticleImage` component automatically resolves teacher images

## Checklist

- [ ] Filename matches the teacher's commonly known name
- [ ] `tradition` and `core_teaching` filled in
- [ ] Biography section written (at least 2 paragraphs)
- [ ] At least one passage wiki-linked
- [ ] `draft: false` when ready to publish
- [ ] Image added (optional but recommended)
