# What's New

WisdomCompiler grows as I revisit old books and discover new ones. Here's what's been added recently.

---

## March 2026

### New Books Added

Twelve new source texts have been added, each with a free link to read the full text online:

- **[Bhagavad Gita](/Books/Bhagavad-Gita)** — The timeless dialogue between Krishna and Arjuna on duty, devotion, and the nature of reality
- **[Tripura Rahasya](/Books/Tripura-Rahasya)** — An ancient Advaitic masterpiece highly recommended by Ramana Maharshi, with 17 passages
- **[Upadesa Saram](/Books/Upadesa-Saram)** — Ramana Maharshi's thirty verses on the essence of spiritual instruction
- **[The Prophet](/Books/The-Prophet)** — Kahlil Gibran's beloved prose-poetry on love, work, joy, sorrow, and freedom
- **[Vivekachudamani](/Books/Vivekachudamani)** — Shankaracharya's "Crest-Jewel of Discrimination," with 6 passages
- **[In Search of the Miraculous](/Books/In-Search-of-the-Miraculous)** — Ouspensky's account of studying with Gurdjieff and the Fourth Way
- **[Conscious Immortality](/Books/Conscious-Immortality)** — Paul Brunton's conversations with Ramana Maharshi
- **[The Nectar of Immortality](/Books/The-Nectar-of-Immortality)** — Nisargadatta Maharaj's direct teachings on consciousness
- **[Dune](/Books/Dune)** — Frank Herbert's science fiction epic, rich with spiritual and philosophical themes
- **[Coming Home to Yourself](/Books/Coming-Home-to-Yourself)** — Osho's meditations on presence and inner stillness
- **[Greater Community Spirituality](/Books/Greater-Community-Spirituality)** — Marshall Vian Summers on a wider view of spiritual purpose
- **[Vidyeshvara Samhita](/Books/Vidyeshvara-Samhita)** — The opening section of the Shiva Purana on devotion to Lord Shiva

### Expanded Teacher Pages

Fourteen teachers now have dedicated pages with biographies, core teachings, lineage context, and portrait images:

- [Ramana Maharshi](/Teachers/Ramana-Maharshi), [Nisargadatta Maharaj](/Teachers/Nisargadatta-Maharaj), [Huang Po](/Teachers/Huang-Po), [Eckhart Tolle](/Teachers/Eckhart-Tolle), [Osho](/Teachers/Osho), [Seung Sahn](/Teachers/Seung-Sahn), and others

Each teacher page links to their recommended starting book and all related passages.

### Quotes Refined

The screenshot passages have been carefully reviewed:

- Source attributions added — each passage now links back to its book
- Text cleaned for readability while preserving the original meaning
- Hundreds of passages given clear, descriptive titles so you can find them more easily

### Daily Wisdom

The homepage now features a **Daily Wisdom** quote that changes each day — a small gift of contemplation each time you visit.

### Source Banners

When reading a passage, you'll now see a banner at the top showing which book or teacher it comes from, with a direct link back to the source.

### Mobile Friendly

The entire site now works well on phones and tablets — read wisdom on the go.
