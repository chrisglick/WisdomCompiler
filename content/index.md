# WisdomCompiler

*One seeker's shrine to the books and teachers that lit the way home.*

---

> "The scriptures belong to those who study them; not to those fools who own them, but do not know their contents."

## What Is This?

Over seven years of intense spiritual seeking, I photographed the passages that stopped me in my tracks — the lines from books and teachers that cracked something open. Advaita Vedanta, Zen, Sufism, manga, scripture, poetry — whatever spoke, I captured it.

These are those screenshots, transcribed and organized. Not a comprehensive library. Not an academic project. Just the passages that one seeker found on the way to realizing their true nature — freely shared in case they help you too.

Each book page includes a link to read the full text online for free where available, so you can go straight from a passage to the source.
