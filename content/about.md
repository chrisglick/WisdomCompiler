# About WisdomCompiler

WisdomCompiler exists for one purpose: to help spiritual seekers find the gurus, scriptures, and sources of wisdom that can transform their understanding.

## The Vision

The world's spiritual traditions contain profound teachings — many of them freely available — but scattered across books, websites, videos, and oral traditions. WisdomCompiler gathers these pointers into one place, organized and searchable, so you can find what you need without wading through noise.

## What You'll Find Here

- **Books & Scriptures** — Notes and pointers to sacred texts from multiple traditions: Hindu, Buddhist, Sufi, Christian, Sikh, and more
- **Teachers** — Profiles of realized masters and their teachings
- **Wisdom Quotes** — Hundreds of passages photographed and transcribed from spiritual classics
- **Media** — YouTube talks, websites, and social media channels worth following

## Principles

1. **Free access** — We only compile and point to freely available resources
2. **Cross-tradition** — Wisdom doesn't belong to any one path
3. **Direct pointing** — Less commentary, more source material
4. **Living library** — This collection grows as new resources are discovered

## Contributing

If you know of free spiritual resources that should be included, or if you spot an error, reach out. This is a community effort.
