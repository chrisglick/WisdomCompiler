# Wisdom Threads

*A thread of truth runs through every tradition. These pages follow that thread — showing how the world's mystics, across centuries and continents, speak the same language of the spirit.*

Each thread collects passages from multiple traditions around a single theme, revealing the perennial philosophy in action.

---

## Threads

- **[[The Thread of Surrender]]** — Ramana Maharshi, Huang Po, Islam, Christianity. The moment the seeker stops seeking and simply opens.
- **[[The Thread of Ego]]** — What all traditions say must dissolve for truth to shine.
- **[[The Thread of Silence]]** — Stillness, mouna, the space between thoughts. Where every tradition points.
