# Wisdom Concepts

*The same truths echo across every tradition. These concept pages collect passages by theme — revealing how Advaita, Zen, Sufism, Christianity, and other paths speak the same language of the spirit.*

---

## The Inner Life

- **[Self-Knowledge](/tags/self-knowledge)** — Who am I? The inquiry at the heart of all traditions. Atma Vichara, self-enquiry, knowing thyself.
- **[Ego](/tags/ego)** — The false self, the I-thought, ahamkara. What must dissolve for truth to shine.
- **[Mind](/tags/mind)** — Thoughts, concepts, maya. The instrument that both binds and liberates.
- **[Silence](/tags/silence)** — Stillness, mouna, peace. The space where wisdom speaks without words.

## The Path

- **[Meditation](/tags/meditation)** — Sadhana, dhyana, samadhi. The practices that turn attention inward.
- **[Surrender](/tags/surrender)** — Letting go, prapatti, submission. The moment the seeker stops seeking.
- **[Love & Devotion](/tags/love-devotion)** — Bhakti, compassion, grace. The heart's path to the divine.
- **[Knowledge & Wisdom](/tags/knowledge-wisdom)** — Jnana, vidya, discernment. Understanding that liberates.

## The Goal

- **[Liberation](/tags/liberation)** — Moksha, nirvana, enlightenment. Freedom from the illusion of separation.
- **[The Divine](/tags/divine)** — God, Brahman, Allah, the Absolute. That which all traditions point toward.

## The Context

- **[Guru & Teacher](/tags/guru-teacher)** — The master, the sage, the guide. Those who light the way.
- **[Service](/tags/service)** — Karma yoga, duty, dharma. Action as spiritual practice.
- **[Scripture](/tags/scripture)** — The teachings, sutras, gitas. Words that carry the transmission.
- **[Nature & Creation](/tags/nature-creation)** — The universe, existence, the cosmos. The divine expressed in form.
- **[Death & Impermanence](/tags/death-impermanence)** — Mortality, dissolution, the transient. What reminds us to seek.
- **[Humility](/tags/humility)** — Simplicity, meekness, modesty. The ground from which wisdom grows.
