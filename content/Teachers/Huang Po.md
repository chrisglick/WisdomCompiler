> *"All the Buddhas and all sentient beings are nothing but the One Mind, beside which nothing exists."*

## Who Was Huang Po?

Huang Po (Huángbò Xīyùn, d. ~850) was a towering Chinese Ch'an (Zen) Buddhist master of the Tang dynasty. Said to be over seven feet tall with a pearl-like protuberance on his forehead, he was as physically striking as his teaching was uncompromising.

He studied under Baizhang Huaihai and became one of the most radical voices in Ch'an history. His most famous student, Linji Yixuan, went on to found the Rinzai school — one of the two surviving Zen lineages.

## Core Teaching

Huang Po taught the doctrine of **One Mind** (一心): there is nothing to attain, nothing to practice, nothing to realize — because you are already the Buddha-nature you seek. All concepts, all distinctions between enlightened and unenlightened, sacred and profane, are themselves the obstacle. His teaching is a sustained demolition of every mental construct the seeker clings to.

## Tradition & Lineage

Ch'an Buddhism → Hongzhou school → Mazu Daoyi → Baizhang Huaihai → **Huang Po** → Linji Yixuan (founder of Rinzai Zen)

## Where to Start

If you're new to Huang Po, begin with [[The Zen Teaching of Huang Po]], translated by John Blofeld. It's short, devastating, and one of the purest expressions of non-dual wisdom in any tradition.
