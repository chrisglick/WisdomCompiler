## Who Was Osho?

Osho (1931-1990), born Rajneesh Chandra Mohan Jain, was one of the most prolific and controversial spiritual teachers of the 20th century. A philosophy professor who experienced enlightenment at age 21, he left academia to become a wandering teacher, eventually building a massive international following.

He gave over 80,000 hours of recorded discourses — commentaries on Zen, Sufism, Taoism, Tantra, Christianity, Hasidism, and Western philosophy — weaving them into a unified vision of total human liberation. His style was deliberately provocative, challenging every convention and orthodoxy.

[Osho International (Official)](https://www.osho.com/)

## Core Teaching

**Total awareness in every dimension of life.** Osho rejected renunciation and asceticism, teaching instead that enlightenment comes through fully embracing life — body, mind, and spirit. His dynamic meditation techniques were designed for modern seekers who couldn't simply sit still. His fundamental message: be total in whatever you do, and awareness will flower naturally.

## Where to Start

For Osho's commentary on other masters, *The Book of Secrets* (on the Vigyan Bhairav Tantra) is extraordinary. For his own voice, *The New Dawn* captures his late-period clarity.
