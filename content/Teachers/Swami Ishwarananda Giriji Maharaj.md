> *"Touch the divine Master seated within you."*

## Who Is Swami Ishwarananda Giriji Maharaj?

Swami Ishwarananda Giriji Maharaj is a revered Indian saint and spiritual master in the tradition of Advaita Vedanta. His teachings carry the warmth of lived devotion -- not abstract philosophy but practical guidance for dissolving the barriers between the seeker and the sacred. He speaks of sadhana not as ritual duty but as the process of calling upon God, a calling that transforms the caller.

## Core Teaching

At the center of his teaching is the conviction that spiritual practice exists to invoke **a strong experiential feeling of belongingness** -- not intellectual belief, not emotional fervor, but a direct, bodily sense of being held by the Divine. He teaches that most people remain trapped in their likes and dislikes, mistaking preferences for identity. The path out is not suppression but redirection: changing one's attitudes, absorbing oneself not in happenings but in the stillness beneath them.

He speaks of "islands of pure consciousness" that arise through sustained practice -- moments when the noise of the mind recedes and what remains is the divine Master seated within. His emphasis on communion with nature reflects a teaching that the sacred is not confined to temples but saturates the living world.
