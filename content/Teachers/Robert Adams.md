## Who Was Robert Adams?

Robert Adams (1928-1997) was an American Advaita teacher who experienced a profound spiritual awakening at the age of fourteen. As a young man, he traveled to India and sat in the presence of Ramana Maharshi at Sri Ramanasramam -- an encounter that confirmed and deepened his own realization. He later spent time with Nisargadatta Maharaj and Paramahansa Yogananda before eventually settling in Sedona, Arizona, where he taught a small circle of devoted students.

Adams spoke softly, laughed easily, and radiated a quiet joy that students found unmistakable. He did not seek publicity or large followings. His teaching was intimate -- a living transmission conducted in small gatherings, often in living rooms.

## Core Teaching

Adams distilled the path into what he called **The Four Principles of Self-Realization**:

1. Everything you see, including your body, is not real as it appears.
2. You are not the body or the mind -- you are absolute awareness.
3. There is nothing to seek and nothing to find -- you are already That.
4. All is well. Everything is unfolding as it should.

These principles are not philosophical propositions to debate but pointers to rest in. His method was self-inquiry in the tradition of Ramana, combined with an emphasis on surrender, silence, and the deep peace that arises when seeking ceases.
