> *"The fool thinks peace comes by controlling the mind. The wise one finds it within."*

## Who Was Ashtavakra?

Ashtavakra ("eight-crooked") is an ancient Vedic sage whose name comes from a legend: while still in his mother's womb, he corrected his father's recitation of the Vedas eight times, and his angry father cursed him to be born with eight bends in his body. Despite his twisted form, he became one of the most brilliant sages in the Indian tradition.

His dialogue with King Janaka of Mithila — the *Ashtavakra Gita* — is among the most radical and direct expressions of non-dual philosophy ever recorded. Where other texts build gradually toward liberation, Ashtavakra declares in the very first verse: you are already free.

## Core Teaching

**You are already the Self.** There is nothing to attain, nothing to practice, nothing to renounce. The very idea that you need liberation is the bondage. Simply recognize what you already are: pure awareness, untouched by the play of the world.

## Tradition & Lineage

Vedic tradition → referenced in the Mahabharata → the *Ashtavakra Gita* stands as one of the crown jewels of Advaita Vedanta.

## Where to Start

The [[Ashtavakra Gita]] itself is short enough to read in one sitting. Let its radical directness wash over you without trying to understand it intellectually.
