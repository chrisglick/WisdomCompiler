Bill Watterson (b. 1958) is an American cartoonist and the creator of *Calvin and Hobbes* (1985-1995), widely regarded as one of the greatest comic strips ever made. He famously refused all merchandising to protect the strip's integrity and lives a deliberately private life. His work overflows with wonder, philosophy, and the wild imagination of childhood.
