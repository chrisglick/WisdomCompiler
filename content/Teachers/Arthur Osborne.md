## Who Was Arthur Osborne?

Arthur Osborne (1906-1970) was a British writer and spiritual seeker who found his home at Sri Ramanasramam in Tiruvannamalai, India. After years of searching across traditions, he recognized in Ramana Maharshi the living embodiment of the truth he had been seeking. He settled permanently at the ashram and devoted his life to preserving and transmitting the Maharshi's teachings.

Osborne became the founding editor of *The Mountain Path*, the ashram's journal, which remains in publication today. His clear, unpretentious prose brought Ramana's teachings to thousands of Western readers who might never have encountered them otherwise.

## Core Teaching

Osborne was not a guru but a devoted transmitter. His contribution was one of faithful clarity -- taking the vast, luminous silence of Ramana's presence and rendering it into English prose that retained its transformative power. His books -- *The Path of Self Knowledge*, *Ramana Maharshi and the Path of Self-Knowledge*, and *The Incredible Sai Baba* -- opened doors for seekers worldwide.

## Where to Start

Begin with [[The Path of Self Knowledge]] for a clear introduction to Ramana's teaching through Osborne's thoughtful lens. For a biographical approach, his *Ramana Maharshi and the Path of Self-Knowledge* remains one of the finest introductions to the sage's life and method.
