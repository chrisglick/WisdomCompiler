Arthur Osborne (1906-1970) was a British writer who settled at Sri Ramanasramam in Tiruvannamalai, India. He edited *The Mountain Path* journal and authored several books that introduced Ramana Maharshi and other Indian spiritual figures to Western readers, including *The Path of Self Knowledge* and *The Incredible Sai Baba*.

## Passages

- [[Only one who is unattached things earthly and heavenly alike can attain to Divine Knowledge]]
- [[all that one is responsible for is the purity and disinterestedness of it]]
