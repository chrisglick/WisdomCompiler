## Who Was Mirza Tahir Ahmad?

Mirza Tahir Ahmad (1928-2003) was the fourth Caliph (*Khalifatul Masih IV*) of the Ahmadiyya Muslim Community, leading the worldwide community from 1982 until his passing. A scholar of extraordinary range, he engaged with evolutionary biology, cosmology, philosophy, and comparative religion with equal fluency, always seeking to demonstrate that authentic faith and rigorous reason are not adversaries but allies.

Under his leadership, the Ahmadiyya community expanded significantly, establishing the first Muslim satellite television channel (MTA International) and strengthening the movement's global humanitarian work. He led from London after being forced into exile from Pakistan, where Ahmadis face legal persecution.

## Core Teaching

His magnum opus, *Revelation, Rationality, Knowledge & Truth*, is a sweeping intellectual defense of religion that takes on the toughest questions: the origin of life, the mechanism of evolution, the nature of consciousness, and the purpose of divine revelation. His argument is not that science must bow to scripture, but that both point toward a single coherent truth when understood deeply enough.

He taught that creation is not arbitrary but purposeful, that the preparatory steps leading to life reveal an intelligence operating through natural law rather than against it.
