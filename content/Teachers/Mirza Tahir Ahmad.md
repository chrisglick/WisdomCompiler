Mirza Tahir Ahmad (1928-2003) was the fourth Caliph (*Khalifatul Masih IV*) of the Ahmadiyya Muslim Community. A scholar and prolific author, his book *Revelation, Rationality, Knowledge & Truth* is an expansive intellectual defense of religion, covering creation, evolution, and the nature of divine revelation.
