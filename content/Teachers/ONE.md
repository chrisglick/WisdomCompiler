## Who Is ONE?

ONE (b. 1986) is a Japanese manga artist known by his pen name alone. He is the creator of *One Punch Man* and *Mob Psycho 100*, both of which explore the nature of true strength, ego, and self-improvement beneath their action-comedy surfaces. He began by self-publishing *One Punch Man* as a webcomic, drawn in a deliberately rough style that belied its philosophical depth.

His stories resonate with a quiet, subversive wisdom about what it means to be genuinely strong. In a genre built on power fantasies, ONE consistently argues that power itself is meaningless -- what matters is kindness, self-knowledge, and the courage to accept one's own limitations.

## Core Teaching

In *Mob Psycho 100*, the protagonist Shigeo Kageyama possesses overwhelming psychic power but is taught by his mentor Reigen that these abilities do not make him special or superior. The series unfolds as a sustained meditation on ego-dissolution: Mob must learn that suppressing emotions is not strength, that accepting pain is not weakness, and that the desire to be "special" is itself the obstacle to genuine connection.

*One Punch Man* approaches the same truth from the opposite direction -- Saitama has become so powerful that nothing challenges him, and the result is not fulfillment but existential emptiness. Strength without meaning is suffering.

## Where to Start

Begin with [[Mob Psycho 100]] for the more spiritually direct work, or [[One Punch Man]] for the philosophical comedy that made ONE famous.
