ONE is a Japanese manga artist known by his pen name. He is the creator of *One Punch Man* and *Mob Psycho 100*, both of which explore the nature of true strength, ego, and self-improvement beneath their action-comedy surfaces. His stories resonate with a quiet, subversive wisdom about what it means to be genuinely strong.
