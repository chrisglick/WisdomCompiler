Seung Sahn (1927-2004) was a Korean Zen master and the first Korean to teach Zen in the West. He founded the Kwan Um School of Zen, now active in over thirty countries. Known for his sharp, humorous teaching style and the question "What is this?", he made Zen accessible without diluting its rigor.

[Kwan Um School of Zen (Official)](https://kwanumzen.org/)
