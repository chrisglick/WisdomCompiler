## Who Is Eckhart Tolle?

Eckhart Tolle (b. 1948) is a German-born spiritual teacher who experienced a profound inner transformation at age twenty-nine. After years of suicidal depression, he awoke one night with the thought "I cannot live with myself any longer" — and in that moment realized there were two selves: the "I" and the "self" it could not live with. The false self dissolved, and he spent the next several years in a state of deep bliss, sitting on park benches.

His books *The Power of Now* and *A New Earth* have introduced millions to the possibility of present-moment awareness and freedom from identification with the mind.

[Eckhart Tolle (Official)](https://eckharttolle.com/)

## Core Teaching

The ego is a mental construct — a voice in your head that you have mistaken for who you are. **Presence** — attention to the Now — dissolves the ego. You don't need to fight it or fix it; you simply need to be present, and it naturally subsides. What remains is the peace of your true nature.
