Eckhart Tolle (b. 1948) is a German-born spiritual teacher and author, best known for *The Power of Now* and *A New Earth*. After a profound inner transformation at age twenty-nine, he devoted his life to teaching present-moment awareness and the dissolution of the egoic mind.
