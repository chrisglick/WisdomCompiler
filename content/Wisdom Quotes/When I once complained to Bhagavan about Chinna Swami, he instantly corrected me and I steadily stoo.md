When I once complained to Bhagavan about Chinna Swami, he instantly corrected me and I steadily stood by that correction all my life in the ashram. Bhagavan curtly asked me, ‘Have you come all the way to sort out lapses in the ashram management? Attend to the business for  which you have come. Find out who from within raises these complaints. Leave the rest to the higher power. Be still.”
