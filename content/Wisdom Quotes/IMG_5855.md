"Come down Master, come down,
To give us your touch, your look, your glorious word.
Enter into us — we are the open house,
Dark, silent, empty and waiting."

"Master!
You are the dust of the path and the feet that walk,
You are the light and you the lid of the eye,
You are life's vigour and the stillness of death,
You are you and I, in one breath."

— Poojya Swamiji Shri Ishwarananda Giri
