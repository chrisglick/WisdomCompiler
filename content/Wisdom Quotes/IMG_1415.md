I thought that in the end, I was nothing more than a grain of existence...

But the moment you returned me to the world.

Rather, I have not been useful at all.

It was an experience that filled me with such ecstasy and freedom, it was frightening.

It was as if the freedom were melting into the seas and the skies.

I can't, even as your travelling master girl...

I was filled with that omnipotent feeling...

I could do anything... become anything...

Your companions may all be incredibly showy, each and every one of them, but...
