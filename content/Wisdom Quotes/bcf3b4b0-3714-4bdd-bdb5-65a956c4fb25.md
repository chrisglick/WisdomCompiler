You can not do anything with the everlasting Truth that is God.  Where there is no doing, there you should recognize him.  Hence it is told that God cannot be approached or tackled. You can only allow Him to approach and tackle you.  That choice is yours, to allow Him or not. Be simple and still, loving and intent ---He  will surely bend down to Lift you up.  Swami Ishwarananda Giri
