Janaka said: 6.1  I am infinite space;  the universe is a jar. This I know. No need to renounce, accept or destroy. 6.2 I am a shoreless ocean; the universe makes waves.  This I know. No need to renounce, accept or destroy. 6.3  I am mother-of-pearl; the universe is the illusion of silver. This I know. No need to renounce, accept or destroy. 6.4 I am in all beings; all beings are in me.  This I know. No need to renounce, accept or destroy.
