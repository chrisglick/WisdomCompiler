If you really want to know what the truth is, the scriptures won't help. Neither will going to the Himalayas be of any help. Only one thing can help: start looking at things without the mind.

Look at the flower and don’t allow the mind to say anything. Just look at it. It is difficult because of an old habit of interpreting. You go on interpreting, and interpretations differ. Interpretations

depend on the mind.
