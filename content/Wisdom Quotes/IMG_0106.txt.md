2 There a centurion's servant, whom his master valued highly, was sick

3 The centurion heard of Jesus and sent some elders of the Jews to

him, asking him to come and heal his servant.

4 When they came to Jesus, they pleaded earnestly with him, “This man

6 So Jesus went with them. He was not far from the house when the centurion sent friends to say to him: “Lord, don’t trouble yourself, for |

7 That is why | did not even consider myself worthy to come to you. But

my servant, ‘Do this,’ and he does it.”

9 When Jesus heard this, he was amazed at him, and turning to the crowd following him, he said, “I tell you, | have not found such great faith

even in Israel.”

10 Then the men who had been sent returned to the house and found
