title: "Man is a vessel, a mask of the Great Actor"
---

The ideal point of view follows from the notion of the openness of all things: there is no actor and no action, though there is acting. The performer is acting only by virtue of his receptivity to the flow of energies coming from the source. The source is not “this” but universal and common to all. Man is a vessel, a mask of the Great Actor, a limb of the Great Dancer.
