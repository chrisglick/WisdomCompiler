title: "The magic of transformation lies in recognizing"

The magic of transformation lies in recognizing that there are forces at play that must be invited into our awareness. Once our “as if” concept of life is expanded experientially to include these forces, we will live into life as though we were a Formula One race car driver. This expansion is the key to presence and will leverage whatever gift you carry that is currently unexpressed or undeveloped.
