34. I am not the individual soul. There are no differences for me. I am not thought, nor am I the mind. I am neither flesh, nor have I bones, nor am I the body with the ego.  35. I am not all. I am transcendent. I am of the nature of Knowledge. I am not all, at any time.  36. I am not dead. I have not another life. I am not “just Consciousness.” I am not. I cannot be spoken of, nor am I “liberated.” I am not “enlightened,” ever.
