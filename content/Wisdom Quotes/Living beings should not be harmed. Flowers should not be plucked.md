That tender leaves should not be cut from trees is also stated in the *sloka* in “Devikalottara Stotra” in *Jnanachara Vicharapadalam*, given below:

Roots should not be pulled out. Leaves should not be pinched. Living beings should not be harmed. Flowers should not be plucked.
