title: "Yes he has come back that was my doing"

Bhagavan said, “Yes, he has come back. That was about fifteen days ago. I have been observing him. He does not speak. So, how then could I ask him ‘What is Pandharpur like? Where is the prasadam, etc.?’ We have to conduct ourselves according to the workings of the minds of others. We are in duty bound to adjust ourselves thus.” People of intelligence examine their own minds. There is no knowing about the minds of others. Bhagavan says that he has to adjust himself according to the desires and intentions of

others! See what a great precept that is!
