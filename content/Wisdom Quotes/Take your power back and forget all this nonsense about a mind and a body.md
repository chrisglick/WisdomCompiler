Do not accept your feeling. Do not accept your thoughts. Watch yourself feeling miserable, and do nothing about it; you can become the witness to it. That will help too. But it’s better to ask, “Why am I feeling miserable?” And realize that you said why am “I” feeling miserable. I. I’m identifying with my body as I. Again a mistake.
