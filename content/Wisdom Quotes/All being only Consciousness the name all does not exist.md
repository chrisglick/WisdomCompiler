25. All being only Consciousness, the name “all’’ never is. Renouncing all forms, be of the certitude that all is Brahman.

26. All is Brahman: that is the Truth. The phenomenal world and prakriti (manifestation), verily, do not exist. Renounce the remembrance of prakriti and resort to the remembrance of Brahman.

27. Then, renouncing even that, be firm in your own nature. Renouncing further this “established nature,” remain only as the Self.

28. | Renouncing the renunciation even, ever leave off the idea of any difference. Surrounding yourself yourself, abide in yourself yourself.
