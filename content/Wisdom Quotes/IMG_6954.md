And every part of the city is fourfold, and every inhabitant fourfold.
And every pot and vessel and garment and utensil of the houses,
And every house, fourfold.

— William Blake

As a prophet in the midst of the “Mills of Satan,” William Blake foresaw—and remembered the Mandala of the fourfold vision of Jerusalem. This is the city of the future which is the eternal city of vision.
