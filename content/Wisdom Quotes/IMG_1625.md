2.2.2. Electric Machine

As explained previously, the electrical machine or integrated MG is coupled to the flywheel, to enable the energy conversion and charging process of the flywheel. The machine, acting as a motor, charges the flywheel by accelerating it and drawing electrical energy from the source. The stored energy on the flywheel is extracted by the same machine, acting as a generator, and hence, the flywheel is slowed down during discharge. Common electrical machines used in FESS are the induction machine (IM), permanent magnet machine (PM), and variable reluctant machine (VRM) [18,23].
