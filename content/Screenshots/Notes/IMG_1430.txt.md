8 Tripura Rahasya

60. Sri Guru! Ocean of Mercy, can anyone drenched with Thy kindness ever be afflicted by ailments, even if destiny so decree?

61. How can the burning pains of illness touch one who is abiding in the refreshing moon of Thy nectar-like kindness?

[Note: The moon is believed to be the store of nectar with which the pitris (departed ancestors) feed themselves.]
