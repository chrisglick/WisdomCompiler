oH WS oA A Hes oA Mot SHOWA II (15-12, firdtora, He 1D

ras ghorhay ras sayjaa mandar ras meethaa ras maas.

the pleasure of horses, the pleasure of a soft bed in a palace, the pleasure of sweet treats and the pleasure of hearty meals

83 on adie & & ufe om foera QU (15-13, firstora, He 1) aytay ras sareer kay kai ghat naam nivaas. ||2||

-these pleasures of the human body are so numerous; how can the Naam, the Name of the Lord, find its dwelling in the heart? ||2||

fas Sfemt ufs uhh A Sfemr users i 5-13, fadtera, 4: D jit boli-ai pat paa-ee-ai so boli-aa parvaan. Those words are acceptable, which, when spoken, bring honor.

feor Sf feawer fe yet Ho MTS II 5-14, faster, H2 1) fikaa bol viguchnaa sun moorakh man ajaan. Harsh words bring only grief. Listen, O foolish and ignorant mind!

a fa aefe A 3d fe fa age SeeE BI (15-14, feta, He 1D) jo tis bhaaveh say bhalay hor ke kahan vakhaan. ||3]|| Those who are pleasing to Him are good. What else is there to be said? ||3]|

fsa nfs feo ufs foo ue ud fie fase ofomr merfe I 5-15, fadtera, oe D

tin mat tin pat tin Dhan palai jin hirdai rahi-aa samaa-ay.

Wisdom, honor and wealth are in the laps of those whose hearts remain permeated with the Lord.

fS6 ot four Ager nies ForfeS ate i (5-16, firdtora, ue 1) tin kaa ki-aa salaahnaa avar su-aali-o kaa-ay.

What praise can be offered to them? What other adornments can be bestowed upon them?

wea sedt aad Tufs ots o STE ISIN (15-16, fadtora, nH: 1) naanak nadree baahray raacheh daan na naa-ay. ||4||4]|

O Nanak, those who lack the Lord's Glance of Grace cherish neither charity nor the Lord's Name. ||4||4]|
