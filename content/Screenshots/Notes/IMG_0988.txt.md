Arunachala means Aruna-achala. Aruna is being, aliveness, and acha/a means ‘unmoved’. When the mind remains unmoved, then it has no other choice but to dissolve itself in the Self, in Self awareness, in other words, that which is called ‘unmoved’. That is what Bhagavan meant when he said, “Everyone finally has to come to Arunachala.” If you want the truth you have to keep the moving mind immobile.
