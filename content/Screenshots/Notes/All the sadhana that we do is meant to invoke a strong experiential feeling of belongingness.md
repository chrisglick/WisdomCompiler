“All the sadhana that we do is meant to invoke a strong experiential feeling of belongingness. The feeling or experience that we belong to the Mother is what we ask of her: nothing else. In fact we do not need to ask of her anything else. Bhakti by itself is such a pure stream that it cleanses us of everything."
