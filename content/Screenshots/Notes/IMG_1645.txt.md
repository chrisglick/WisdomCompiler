32.  The world, which is afflicted with sorrow, which is considered as a creation separate from the feet of Lord Siva, which is full of mental modes, which is not blissful with the thoughts of the Supreme, and which is subject to the sextet of states, will [upon one’s] abandoning of all modes, dissolve, along with the eyes and minds and words of its beings in the dense, immaculate mass of Mahesvara, like the river merging indivisibly in the ocean.
