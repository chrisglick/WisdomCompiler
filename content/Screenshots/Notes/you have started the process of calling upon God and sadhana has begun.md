Call Of Divine. When you speak of Samvit Sadhana and identify yourselves as sadhakas, you have started the process of calling upon God and sadhana has begun. When eventually the Call of God comes to you you will become like Meera. Pujya Swami Shri Ishwarananda GiriJi Maharaj
