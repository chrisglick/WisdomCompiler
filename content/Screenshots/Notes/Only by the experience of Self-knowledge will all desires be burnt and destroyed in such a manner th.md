text::37. Only by the experience of Self-knowledge will all desires be burnt and destroyed in such a manner that they can never again revive. Nobody has ever overcome the power of desires merely by fighting and struggling for any number of years against the wandering nature of the five senses.  38. Know that this indeed is the reason why our Father, Guru Ramana, always gave the advice “Know yourself’ and unfailingly taught the path of Self-inquiry as the most powerful practice (sad- hana), and as the only weapon to destroy all the de- sires existing within us.
