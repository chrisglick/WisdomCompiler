The vibrations of the person preparing the food enter into food cooked over fire. A Sanskrit mantra which can be used for preparing food is:  OM ANNAM BRAHMA RASO BISHMUR BHOKTA DEBO JANARDANAHI AWAEM GYANTWA TO YO BHUNKT ANN DOSHORN LIPYATE  which means: Food is Brahma. Its rasa (juice) is Vishnu. The whole world (Life Being) is its user. Having this thought makes ineffective the evils connected with this food.
