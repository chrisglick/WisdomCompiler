text::17.19  Though he may perform actions, the man of Knowledge  does not act.  Desires extinguished,  free of thoughts of “I’ and “mine,” he knows with absolute certainty that nothing exists.  17.20  The sage is free.  His empty mind no longer projects delusion, dreaming, dullness.  This state is indescribable.
