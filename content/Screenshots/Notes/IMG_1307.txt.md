hese are the seven states of ignorance. | shall now tell

the seven stages of knowledge which bestow Liberati 149. elders have analyzed them as: Subheccha: desire for Truth; Vicharana: investigation into the Truth; Tanumanasi: pure and attenuated mind; 4.

Sattvapatti: the Realization of the Truth;

5. Asamshakti: a detached outlook on the universe and its

contents;

6. Padarthabhavani: untainted awareness of Self; Turiya: the highest and indescribable state.

150 & 151. To explain further:

1. To wean away unedifying associations and to desire knowledge of the Supreme is the first plane called

ubheccha.

2. To associate with enlightened Sages, learn from them

and reflect on the Truth, is called investigatio

3. To be free from desires by meditating on the Truth

with faith, is the attenuation of the mind.
